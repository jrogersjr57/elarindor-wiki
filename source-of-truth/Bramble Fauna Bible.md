That entry needs to preserve our bee decisions: workers approximately 2–2.5 inches long; fundamentally recognizable honeybee anatomy; two pairs of relatively broad wings with strong venation and subtle natural iridescence; robust flight thorax; tawny/golden/copper thoracic fur; dark and amber/copper abdominal banding; pronounced pollen-gathering structures and branched hairs; somewhat longer tongue; and a robust, purposeful rather than monstrous appearance.

Colonies retain queens, workers, and drones but also have specialized soldier-workers. Soldiers are more robust defensive workers but can perform ordinary colony labor when defensive work is unnecessary.

Defense is intimidation-first. Guards and soldiers use conspicuous posture, wing vibration/deeper warning sound, warning flights, mandibles, grappling or physical displacement before stinging. Stinging remains fatal to the bee. Venom is deliberately potent, making the final defensive act consequential.
