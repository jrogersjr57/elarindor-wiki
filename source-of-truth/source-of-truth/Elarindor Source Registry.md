# Elarindor Source Registry

This registry identifies the current written and procedural sources of
truth for Elarindor and records the subject each source owns.

The registry is an **authority index**, not an independent lore Bible.
It does not create new canon by summarizing or listing a source. Canon
status and substantive lore remain in the source documents themselves.

Use this registry together with \[\[Elarindor Loremaster Guidelines\]\],
\[\[Elarindor Visual Reference Registry\]\], and \[\[Elarindor
Development History\]\].

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Authority Rules

When sources overlap, apply the source-precedence rules in \[\[Elarindor
Loremaster Guidelines\]\].

In summary:

1.  The creator's explicit current decision has highest authority.
2.  Canon outranks Working Canon, Concept, and TBD where they directly
    conflict.
3.  Within the same classification, the source specifically assigned to
    a subject controls that subject within its scope.
4.  Specialized sources and references control only their delegated
    scope.
5.  Recency breaks ties only between sources of equivalent authority and
    scope, or where supersession is explicit.
6.  Rejected or Superseded material has no current authority unless
    deliberately reopened.
7.  Unresolved conflicts between equivalent authoritative sources must
    be surfaced to the creator.
8.  Rejected or Superseded material is preserved in \[\[Elarindor
    Development History\]\], not retained as obsolete lore inside active
    subject sources.

# 2. Core Project Sources

## Elarindor.md

-   **Type:** World / Map Framework
-   **Status:** Working Canon with explicitly provisional map material
-   **Authoritative Scope:** World identity; Earth-sized world premise;
    current major-continent framework; working continental scale; broad
    map-development intent; distinction between author knowledge and
    in-world knowledge.
-   **Does Not Automatically Control:** Exact coastlines, exact terrain
    boundaries, settlement locations, roads, generated labels, or other
    map details explicitly marked provisional.
-   **Related Sources:** \[\[Elarindor Visual Reference Registry\]\]
-   **Supersedes:** None established.
-   **Superseded By:** None established.
-   **Notes:** The map represents one major continental area, not the
    whole world.

## Elarindor Loremaster Guidelines.md

-   **Type:** Project Governance / Continuity Protocol
-   **Status:** Authoritative project procedure
-   **Authoritative Scope:** Source use, canon classification, source
    precedence, brainstorming status, continuity protection, Bible
    scope, worldbuilding method, source-of-truth updates, supersession
    procedure, uncertainty handling, and creator authority.
-   **Does Not Automatically Control:** Substantive setting lore owned
    by a subject-specific Bible.
-   **Related Sources:** \[[Elarindor Source
    Registry](#elarindor-source-registry)\], \[\[Elarindor Concept Art
    Protocol\]\], \[\[Elarindor Visual Reference Registry\]\],
    \[\[Elarindor Development History\]\]
-   **Supersedes:** None established.
-   **Superseded By:** None established.

## Elarindor Concept Art Protocol.md

-   **Type:** Visual Development Protocol
-   **Status:** Authoritative project procedure
-   **Authoritative Scope:** Elarindor concept-art workflow; LOCKED /
    VARIABLE / UNSPECIFIED classification; AVR use; generator-access
    gate; controlled variation; fidelity evaluation; visual drift; image
    canonization procedure.
-   **Does Not Automatically Control:** Substantive biological,
    cultural, architectural, geographic, or character lore except
    insofar as it governs how authoritative lore is preserved during
    visual development.
-   **Related Sources:** \[\[Elarindor Visual Reference Registry\]\],
    \[\[Elarindor Loremaster Guidelines\]\]
-   **Supersedes:** Earlier visual-generation practices where they
    conflict with this protocol.
-   **Superseded By:** None established.

## Elarindor Visual Reference Registry.md

-   **Type:** Visual Authority Registry
-   **Status:** Authoritative registry
-   **Authoritative Scope:** Identity, status, role, scope, fidelity
    requirement, and supersession status of creator-approved
    Authoritative Visual References.
-   **Does Not Automatically Control:** Lore not contained in the
    registered scope of an AVR; generator access to an image.
-   **Related Sources:** \[\[Elarindor Concept Art Protocol\]\],
    \[\[Elarindor Development History\]\]
-   **Supersedes:** Informal assumptions that an image is authoritative
    merely because it exists in project files.
-   **Superseded By:** None established.

## Elarindor Development History.md

-   **Type:** Project Development History / Rejected and Superseded
    Material Archive
-   **Status:** Authoritative development record; not a source of
    current setting lore
-   **Authoritative Scope:** Records material explicitly Rejected or
    Superseded during Elarindor's development, including its originating
    subject/source, earlier direction, replacement where established,
    and relevant development context.
-   **Does Not Automatically Control:** Current setting lore, current
    canon status, or the authority of active subject Bibles and
    references.
-   **Related Sources:** \[\[Elarindor Loremaster Guidelines\]\],
    \[[Elarindor Source Registry](#elarindor-source-registry)\],
    \[\[Elarindor Visual Reference Registry\]\]
-   **Supersedes:** None established.
-   **Superseded By:** None established.
-   **Notes:** Rejected or Superseded material is moved out of active
    sources and preserved here. Material recorded here has no current
    lore authority unless the creator deliberately reopens or re-adopts
    it.

# 4. Visual Sources

Individual AVRs are not duplicated here.

Their authority, scope, fidelity requirements, and supersession
relationships are maintained in \[\[Elarindor Visual Reference
Registry\]\].

A visual reference does not gain authority merely by appearing in a
Bible, Knowledge, a conversation, or project storage. It must be
explicitly approved and registered, or otherwise explicitly established
by the creator.

When an AVR is rejected or superseded, update the Visual Reference
Registry and preserve the obsolete visual direction or supersession
record in \[\[Elarindor Development History\]\] as appropriate.

# 5. Future Source Registration

When a new source of truth is created, add an entry containing:

-   **Type**
-   **Status**
-   **Authoritative Scope**
-   **Does Not Automatically Control**
-   **Related Sources**
-   **Supersedes**
-   **Superseded By**
-   **Notes**, when needed

Register the source when it is added, renamed, superseded, or when its
authoritative scope materially changes.

When lore within an active source is explicitly rejected or superseded,
remove the obsolete material from that active source and preserve it in
\[\[Elarindor Development History\]\]. The active source should describe
the current state of Elarindor rather than serving as the archive for
its own obsolete versions.

Do not infer a new source's authority merely from its title. Define its
scope deliberately.

# 6. Known Authority Clarifications

## Valdari Pigmentation

The [[Valdari Race Bible]] establishes a broad natural Valdari skin-pigmentation range extending from pale complexions through varied earthen hues to very deep or ebony complexions.

The exact frequencies and regional distributions of these complexions remain **TBD**.

Natural hair coloration likewise varies among Valdari populations. Established colors include blonde, light brown, dark brown, black, moss green, and silver, while their exact regional distributions remain under development.

Individual character coloration does not establish the frequency or distribution of a trait across the wider Valdari population.

## Elarindor Map References

`Elarindor_Initial_Concept.png` is currently the registered
map/geographic AVR in \[\[Elarindor Visual Reference Registry\]\].

`Elarindor_Final_Concept.png` is referenced by \[\[Elarindor\]\] but is
not currently registered as an AVR. Its filename does not itself make it
newer in authority, supersede the registered AVR, or define its scope.

If the creator intends the Final Concept to replace, supplement, or
divide authority with the Initial Concept, that decision remains to be
explicitly established and recorded in the Visual Reference Registry.
