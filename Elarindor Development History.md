> [!info] Purpose **Status:** Authoritative Project Development Record
> --- Non-Authoritative for Current Lore
>
> This document records Elarindor lore and design directions that the
> creator has explicitly replaced or rejected when retaining that
> history is useful for continuity protection.
>
> This is **not** a source of current setting lore. Material recorded
> here must never override, supplement, or be treated as an alternative
> to the current authoritative source unless the creator deliberately
> reopens it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Use Rules

1.  Active Sources of Truth should contain the current state of Elarindor, not obsolete versions retained for historical purposes.
2.  When the creator explicitly replaces or rejects established material, remove the obsolete material from the active source.
3.  Record a change here only when doing so helps prevent accidental reintroduction or preserves useful development context.
4.  Do not copy removed passages wholesale. Summarize only enough to identify the former direction.
5.  Record the creator's reason for a change only when that reason is actually established. Otherwise use **Reason not recorded**.
6.  The current authoritative source always controls the subject.
7.  A former direction may be reconsidered only when the creator deliberately reopens it.

# 2. Valdari Development History

## Valdari Ear Anatomy

-   **Original location:** [[Worldbuilding/Elarindor/AI Sources of Truth/Valdari Race Bible]], Ears
-   **Former direction:** Valdari ears were described generically as slightly pointed / generic pointed fantasy ears.
-   **Current direction:** Valdari ears are fundamentally human-like in structure, with the upper portion gently sweeping backward into a subtle windswept or leaf-like silhouette.
-   **Current authority:** [[Worldbuilding/Elarindor/AI Sources of Truth/Valdari Race Bible]]

## Valdari Stature Revision

- **Original location:** [[Valdari Race Bible]], Stature, Vaelyn's Individual Body Type, and Visual Canon
- **Former direction:** Adult Valdari were generally described as approximately 2'10"--3'6", with a typical female around 3'3", a typical male around 3'5", and Vaelyn Thistletide around 3'3".
- **Current direction:** Typical adult Valdari females stand approximately 3'2"--3'4", typical adult males approximately 3'4"--3'6", and absolute adult height limits remain TBD. Vaelyn Thistletide stands approximately 3'4¾" and is unusually tall for a Valdari woman while remaining within the typical male range.
- **Current authority:** [[Valdari Race Bible]]
- **Reason:** Revised after comparative visual development to better establish population-level stature variation and Vaelyn's individual height.
- **Status:** Replaced

---

## Valdari Height Revision - August 29, 2026

Comparative scale testing against average human demonstrated that the previous Valdari height standards placed the population somewhat taller than desired for their intended visual identity.

**New Canon**
- Adult Valdari range: **2'10" - 3'4"**
- Average adult female: **~3'0"**
- Average adult male: **~3'2"**
- Vaelyn Thistletide: **3'2"**

Previous figures - including the 2'10" - 3'6" population range, 3'3" female/3'5" male averages, the later 3'2" - 3'6" working range, and Vaelyn's 3'4 3/4" working height - are superseded.
