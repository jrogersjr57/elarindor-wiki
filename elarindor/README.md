# Elarindor

A high-fantasy world currently in active development.

---

## Overview

At its core, Elarindor is a space for discovery - where concepts are develop, tested, and refined over time..

This directory contains all lore, concepts, and structured content specific to Elarindor.

---

## Structure

- Races – Intelligent peoples and their traits (e.g., Valdari, Murnari)
- Cultures – Traditions, lifestyles, and societal structures
- Locations – Regions, landmarks, and settlements
- Magic – Systems and expressions of magic
- Pantheon - Dieties, belief systems, and religious structures
- Creatures – Beasts, constructs, and other entities
- History – Events shaping the world
- Notes – World-specific ideas in development

---

## Areas of Development

The following concepts are currently being explored and may shape the direction of Elarindor over time:
- Valdari – Forest-dwelling, magically attuned culture with deep traditions  
- Murnari – Nomadic caravan society tied to movement and storytelling  
- Golems – Material-based constructs with defined roles and scale  
- Large-scale World Features - Concepts involving massive environmental structures and forces

These ideas are not final and may evolve as the world develops.

---

## Current Focus

The following areas are currently being developed or refined:
- Races - Understand the people of Elarindor  
- Magic/Pantheon - What does magic involve do? Does it affect the world or the world affect it? Who do the inhabitants please to for intervention and salvation?  

This section will evolve and change focus as different ideas are explored or finalized.
---

## Status

Elarindor is an active, evolving project. Content ranges from early drafts to more developed lore and may change as ideas are refined.
