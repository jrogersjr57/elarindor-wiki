The following are current working decisions. They should guide development, but remain subject to revision until established as canon:
- **Elarindor is the world**, approximately Earth-sized; this image represents one major continent/continental area, not the whole world.
- **Current continental scale:** retain the present **0–1000 mile scale bar** as the working scale.
- **Overall size:** the main continent is intended to be roughly comparable in scale to the **continental United States**, rather than Europe-sized or a small fantasy island.
- The current **coastline and landmasses are provisional**. They establish the general shape and proportions but may be redrawn.
- **Cross-document use of “Bramble”:** other Elarindor Bibles may use **Bramble** as the current working identifier for this continent. Such usage identifies the same continental setting but does **not** promote “Bramble” to a final canonical name.
- **Regional names, settlement names, sea names, and most other labels are provisional** unless we've independently established them elsewhere in Elarindor's lore.
- **Terrain placement is conceptual.** The current map establishes the idea that the continent contains northern cold regions, major mountain systems, large forests, plains, jungles, arid regions, islands, etc., but their precise boundaries can change.
- The finished map should maintain a believable **continental variety of climates and terrain**, rather than treating each current colored region as an immutable political/geographical boundary.
- The current concept image should be used as a **visual reference when rebuilding**, not something that has to be traced exactly.
- Reconstruction can be done with CC3+, Photoshop, Procreate, Affinity Designer/iPad, or another suitable cartography application.

## Map Design & Reconstruction Notes

AI is being used and to preserve design intent consider the following:
- **The map is not a world map.** It is currently a concept for one major continent/continental area of the Earth-sized world of Elarindor.
- **Continental scale:** approximately 2,500–3,000 miles across, using the continental United States as the working real-world size reference.
- **Geographic intent:** the continent should be large enough to support extensive wilderness, long mountain ranges, enormous forests, plains, wetlands, arid areas, jungles/tropical areas, and substantial climate differences.
- **Climate intent:** the far north currently represents genuinely cold/subarctic or arctic territory rather than simply mountains painted white.
- **Islands:** multiple substantial offshore islands/island groups are part of the current concept, but their shapes, number, placement and names are not canonical.
- **Rivers:** rivers should ultimately be designed intentionally according to terrain rather than simply copied from the AI artwork.
- **Settlements/roads:** their current positions and names are placeholders. The final map should place them according to the cultures, geography, trade, rivers, resources and history we develop.
- **No hex grid on the final artistic map.** We experimented with one and ultimately removed it. A grid could still be used separately for measurement/gameplay if desired.
- **Cartographic appearance:** antique illustrated fantasy-atlas aesthetic, with painted/pictorial terrain, parchment presentation, decorative border, compass rose, legend and mileage scale. This is a visual direction, not a requirement that the final map exactly reproduce the concept artwork.
- **Production workflow:** the AI image is a reference/background for reconstruction. Geography should eventually exist as independently editable components—coastlines, terrain, mountains, forests, rivers, roads, settlements, labels, islands and decorative elements.
- **iPad is a viable reconstruction platform.** We discussed Affinity Designer as particularly attractive because it would allow vector coastline/rivers/roads and editable layers without requiring a desktop drawing tablet; Procreate would be more appropriate for hand-painted finishing. CC3+ remains useful for accurate cartography and later regional/settlement maps.
- **Concept-art status:** AI-generated labels should **never automatically become canon**. A name or feature becomes canonical only when you deliberately establish it in the Elarindor documentation.

> When the map is rebuilt, geography should be reconsidered for plausible climate, watersheds, mountain formation, rivers, biomes, settlement placement and travel rather than reproducing AI-generated geography exactly.
