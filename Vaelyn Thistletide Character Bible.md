# 1. Core Identity

**Name:** Vaelyn Thistletide\
**Species:** Valdari\
**Height:** Approximately **3'2" (97 cm)** --- Working Canon\
**Primary Developed Vocations:** Druid; later katana swordswoman\
**Current Development Status:** Active character development

Vaelyn is an individual Valdari and must not be treated as a universal
template for Valdari women.

She is unusually quick to understand principles and new ideas. This
aptitude does not eliminate the need for practice, physical
conditioning, repetition, or lived experience.

Vaelyn's life develops along two major disciplines: **druidry** and
**swordsmanship**. Druidry is learned first. Swordsmanship later becomes
the discipline with which she most strongly identifies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Physical Identity

## 2.1 Established Individual Traits

Vaelyn stands approximately **3'2" (97 cm)**. She is taller than the
average Valdari woman but is not unusually large for a Valdari.

She possesses fully mature adult Valdari anatomy and proportions. Her
youthful appearance must never be interpreted as childlike anatomy.

Her established visual identity includes:

-   golden-blonde hair, characteristically worn in a long braid;
-   emerald-green eyes with a subtle gold ring around the pupil;
-   freckles;
-   a youthful adult face;
-   mature feminine proportions;
-   Valdari ear anatomy as established by the \[\[Valdari Race Bible\]\]
    and applicable AVR;
-   and her established individual body type and proportions as
    controlled by her registered visual references.

Detailed visual identity is governed by \[\[Elarindor Visual Reference
Registry\]\] and the applicable Authoritative Visual References rather
than by this prose summary.

## 2.2 Martial Conditioning

**Status: Working Canon**

Long-term sword training changes Vaelyn's physical conditioning without
changing her fundamental body type.

Over years of training she becomes increasingly lean, toned, athletic,
balanced, and physically efficient. Conditioning is especially apparent
through her:

-   forearms and grip;
-   shoulders and upper back;
-   core;
-   thighs and calves;
-   posture;
-   balance;
-   and economy of movement.

She does not become bulky. Her established feminine body shape and
individual proportions remain recognizably Vaelyn's.

These are **Vaelyn-specific developments** and must not be generalized
into Valdari population traits.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Aptitude and Learning

**Status: Working Canon**

Vaelyn learns and comprehends new principles unusually quickly.

Her aptitude is not equivalent to instant mastery.

Druidic concepts come comparatively easily to her, although practice
remains necessary. Swordsmanship exposes the limits of intellectual
understanding more directly: Vaelyn can understand a principle rapidly,
but her body must still learn it through repetition, conditioning,
timing, balance, and disciplined practice.

This distinction becomes important to her relationship with
swordsmanship. The sword gives her a discipline that intelligence alone
cannot shortcut.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Family Background

## 4.1 Alchemical Tradition

**Status: Working Canon**

Vaelyn comes from a family with an alchemical tradition.

Her family hoped that after her druidic education she would eventually
pursue the family's alchemical path. This was a **family expectation**,
not a Valdari cultural requirement.

Growing up around a family occupation can provide extensive practical
familiarity without determining a young Valdari's eventual profession,
consistent with \[\[Valdari Culture & Daily Life Bible\]\].

The exact nature of the Thistletide family's alchemical work, its
history, its economic role, and Vaelyn's degree of formal alchemical
training remain **TBD**.

# 5. Druidic Path

## 5.1 Training

**Status: Working Canon**

Vaelyn begins formal druidic training during her pre-adult years,
approximately in the **20--30** age range.

She learns unusually quickly but still requires practical experience and
training.

By approximately age **30**, Vaelyn has completed or qualified in her
druidic training sufficiently to pursue another demanding discipline.

The exact normal duration, institutional structure, curriculum,
qualifications, and terminology of Valdari druidic education remain
**TBD**.

## 5.2 Continuing Ability

**Status: Working Canon**

Vaelyn does **not** lose or abandon her druidic knowledge when she
becomes a swordswoman.

She retains her druidic abilities, spells, practical knowledge, and
understanding of the natural world.

Over time, however, she increasingly chooses the sword in situations
where she judges physical action to be the better tool. This is a matter
of preference, judgment, and identity---not because her magic weakens,
fails, or becomes unavailable.

Her personal development therefore moves broadly from:

**a druid learning swordsmanship**

toward:

**a swordswoman who also possesses substantial druidic knowledge and
ability.**

A druidic staff may therefore be appropriate to depictions of Vaelyn
during relevant earlier periods of her life, but it is not her defining
weapon after her martial development and receipt of her commissioned
katana.

# 6. Meeting the Swordmaster

**Status: Working Canon**

During Vaelyn's druidic training, a dangerous situation brings her into
contact with the swordswoman who will later become her Swordmaster.

The Swordmaster saves Vaelyn using **katana swordsmanship**.

Vaelyn is fascinated not primarily by violence, but by the discipline
displayed through timing, footwork, control, precision, and economy of
movement.

The Swordmaster demonstrates or teaches Vaelyn a very small number of
initial principles or skills. Vaelyn's ability to understand them
unusually quickly catches the Swordmaster's attention.

Vaelyn asks to train under her.

The Swordmaster refuses to interfere with Vaelyn's existing druidic
apprenticeship and requires her to finish what she has already begun
before returning to seek sword instruction.

Vaelyn later does so.

The exact location, danger, circumstances, dialogue, and sequence of
this first meeting remain **TBD** unless separately established.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. The Swordmaster

## 7.1 Established Direction

**Status: Working Canon**

Vaelyn's Swordmaster is a **female, human-sized swordswoman** who
practices a martial tradition using a literal **katana**.

She is substantially taller than Vaelyn because of Vaelyn's Valdari
stature.

She did not originally intend to take Vaelyn as an apprentice. Vaelyn's
unusual aptitude first attracts her attention, but Vaelyn's discipline
and commitment ultimately justify the apprenticeship.

## 7.2 Identity

**Status: TBD**

The following remain unresolved:

-   her name;
-   exact ancestry/species;
-   age;
-   homeland;
-   formal title;
-   exact martial institution;
-   personal history;
-   and the complete appearance of her own katana.

Her own weapon is a **katana**, but it is a separate weapon and is **not
required to resemble Vaelyn's commissioned katana**.

## 7.3 Lost-Culture Direction

**Status: Concept --- Not Yet Canon**

A major developing possibility is that the Swordmaster is the last
surviving member, last complete bearer, or final practitioner of a
culture or martial tradition that is disappearing or already effectively
extinct.

Under this concept, she had accepted that the tradition would end with
her. Vaelyn's aptitude and, more importantly, her sustained discipline
eventually cause the Swordmaster to reconsider that assumption.

Possible consequences include the Swordmaster gradually teaching Vaelyn
not only swordsmanship but terminology, etiquette, weapon care,
philosophy, history, stories, customs, and other cultural knowledge.

The following remain **TBD**:

-   whether the Swordmaster is literally the last biological survivor of
    her people;
-   whether she is instead the last complete bearer of the tradition;
-   what destroyed or dispersed the culture;
-   how much Vaelyn directly witnesses versus learns through testimony;
-   and which elements of the culture survive outside the Swordmaster.

This material must not be treated as established history until
deliberately adopted.

# 8. Swordsmanship Apprenticeship

## 8.1 Beginning

**Status: Working Canon**

At approximately age **30**, after completing her druidic training,
Vaelyn returns to the Swordmaster and asks again to be taught.

The Swordmaster accepts her.

Vaelyn's rapid comprehension helps her learn principles quickly, but
physical mastery still requires years of disciplined repetition.

## 8.2 First Decade

**Status: Working Canon**

Vaelyn trains in swordsmanship for approximately **ten years** before
her family learns of it.

During this period, roughly ages **30--40**, she keeps the training
secret from them.

Her secrecy arises from her reluctance to tell her family about the path
she has chosen, especially given their hope that she would eventually
pursue the family's alchemical tradition. Swordsmanship itself is not
established as forbidden by Valdari society.

## 8.3 Second Decade

**Status: Working Canon**

After her family learns of her training, Vaelyn continues openly for
approximately another decade.

By approximately age **50**, she has trained under her Swordmaster for
about **twenty years**.

Her Swordmaster then recognizes Vaelyn as having reached **mastery**.

This occurs around the same age that Valdari reach recognized adulthood,
but the two milestones are independent. Sword mastery is not a Valdari
coming-of-age requirement, and Valdari adulthood does not depend upon
professional qualification.

# 9. Initial Training Sword

**Status: Working Canon**

No existing katana is initially suited to Vaelyn's small stature.

Her Swordmaster therefore finds the closest practical training weapon
available. It is usable but **slightly heavier than ideal** for Vaelyn.

It is not so heavy that it forces incorrect mechanics or makes proper
training impossible.

Vaelyn trains with this heavier weapon for approximately **five years**
before receiving her commissioned katana.

The prolonged use of the slightly heavier training weapon contributes to
the development of her:

-   grip and forearms;
-   shoulders and upper back;
-   core;
-   hips and legs;
-   balance;
-   and whole-body sword mechanics.

By the later part of this period, Vaelyn has adapted sufficiently that
the training weapon moves naturally in her hands.

This conditioning remains when she transitions to her purpose-built
katana.

# 10. Vaelyn's Commissioned Katana

## 10.1 Identity

**Status: Working Canon**

Vaelyn's characteristic personal weapon is a **literal katana**
commissioned specifically for her by her Swordmaster.

It is not a generic Valdari weapon and does not establish a Valdari
martial tradition.

It is also not merely a miniature decorative copy of a human-sized
sword. It is a functional katana proportioned specifically for Vaelyn's
approximately 3'2" body, including her:

-   hand size;
-   arm length;
-   reach;
-   draw length;
-   strength;
-   and two-handed sword technique.

After receiving it, the katana becomes Vaelyn's characteristic weapon.

## 10.2 Commission

**Status: Working Canon**

At approximately **year three** of Vaelyn's sword training, the
Swordmaster determines that Vaelyn's commitment justifies commissioning
a proper weapon for her.

The katana takes approximately **one to two years** to complete.

Vaelyn therefore continues training with the slightly heavier training
sword while her personal weapon is being made and receives the completed
katana at approximately **year five** of her apprenticeship.

## 10.3 Materials and Construction

**Status: Working Canon**

The katana does **not** rely upon a special lightweight magical metal to
accommodate Vaelyn's stature.

Its smith uses the same familiar, traditional materials with which he
ordinarily works.

The sword's effectiveness in Vaelyn's hands comes from proper design,
craftsmanship, fit, and Vaelyn's conditioning and technique rather than
from an artificially weightless material.

Any magical properties of the completed weapon remain **TBD**.

## 10.4 Visual Identity

**Status: Canonized Visual Design / governed by AVR**

Vaelyn's katana has a fixed visual identity.

Whenever the sword is materially visible in concept art, it must be
**the same weapon**, faithfully reconstructed from its registered
Authoritative Visual Reference in \[\[Elarindor Visual Reference
Registry\]\].

Its established visual identity includes:

-   an unmistakably curved, single-edged katana blade;
-   the established blade profile and proportions;
-   its specific tsuba and fittings;
-   its ornate pale/ivory carved hilt;
-   the established sculptural carving arrangement and terminal
    treatment;
-   and a hilt proportioned to permit Vaelyn's proper separated
    two-handed katana grip.

The sword must not be replaced from image to image by a generic katana,
an approximate ivory-hilted sword, or a newly invented variation.

Scene-dependent presentation may naturally vary with angle, perspective,
lighting, partial occlusion, whether the sword is drawn or sheathed, and
the action being performed. These variations do not authorize
redesigning the weapon.

The exact cultural meanings of individual carved motifs remain **TBD**
unless separately established.

## 10.5 Transition from Training Weapon

**Status: Working Canon**

Because Vaelyn has spent approximately five years training with a
slightly heavier weapon, her commissioned katana initially feels
dramatically quicker and more responsive.

The sword is not unnaturally light. Vaelyn herself has been conditioned
by the heavier trainer.

The change requires some recalibration. Her established strength and
movement can initially produce more response than expected from the
properly fitted weapon, requiring renewed attention to timing,
precision, and control.

# 11. The Master Smith

**Status: Working Canon**

Vaelyn's katana is created by an aging master smith commissioned by her
Swordmaster.

He is a master of his craft.

Because Vaelyn requires an unusually scaled weapon, the commission
involves more than simply reducing ordinary dimensions. The smith
considers Vaelyn's hands, reach, draw, movement, and intended two-handed
technique.

The commission takes approximately **one to two years**, allowing for
careful design, testing, refinement, and rejection of work that does not
meet his standard.

The completed katana is the **finest weapon he has made** and represents
the culmination of his craft.

He recognizes it as his **last sword**, with retirement and age being
the current intended reason rather than an established imminent death.

The exact identity, ancestry, culture, location, workshop, forging
process, and division of labor involved in carving the ivory hilt remain
**TBD**.

# 12. Family Discovery

## 12.1 Established Framework

**Status: Working Canon**

Vaelyn's family eventually learns that she has been secretly training in
swordsmanship, approximately **ten years** into her apprenticeship.

Their central emotional difficulty is not that swordsmanship is
forbidden. It is that Vaelyn concealed such a major part of her life
from them and has chosen a path different from the alchemical future
they had hoped for her.

Her family ultimately accepts her chosen path.

## 12.2 Revelation Incident

**Status: Concept --- Not Yet Canon**

One explored possibility is that the secret is revealed during a
practical danger involving Vaelyn and members of her family.

Under this concept:

-   Vaelyn responds to a dangerous animal or comparable physical threat;
-   her druidic magic does not fail;
-   she chooses the sword because she judges it the better tool in that
    moment;
-   her family witnesses the calm, posture, movement, timing, and
    competence of someone who has clearly trained for years;
-   and the incident causes them to reinterpret the physical
    conditioning and movement changes they have noticed in Vaelyn over
    time.

Her family would not necessarily recognize the katana, know the
Swordmaster's title, or understand the foreign martial tradition on
sight.

The exact incident, animal, location, participants, dialogue, and
outcome remain unresolved.

# 13. Relationship Between Druidry and Swordsmanship

**Status: Working Canon**

Vaelyn's martial development does not invalidate her druidic identity or
abilities.

Instead, her preferred response to danger changes with experience.

Earlier in life, druidic magic is the discipline through which she is
principally trained. As her swordsmanship develops, Vaelyn increasingly
judges the blade to be the better solution to many immediate physical
threats.

Her preference for swordsmanship is not evidence that:

-   her magic has weakened;
-   she has lost access to druidic abilities;
-   druidry is inferior;
-   or swordsmanship is culturally superior.

It reflects Vaelyn's personal aptitude, interests, judgment, and chosen
identity.

By the mature stage of this development, the most accurate shorthand is:

> **Vaelyn is a swordswoman who knows and retains druidic magic.**

Her characteristic weapon is her commissioned katana.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. Preservation of the Swordmaster's Tradition

**Status: Concept --- Not Yet Canon**

If the Swordmaster is established as the final bearer of a dying or
extinct culture, Vaelyn may eventually choose to preserve what was
entrusted to her.

This possibility fits Vaelyn's Valdari background without making
cultural preservation an automatic racial destiny. Valdari longevity,
accumulated knowledge, memory, craftsmanship, learning, and tradition
could make Vaelyn unusually capable of preserving knowledge across
centuries, but **Vaelyn must choose that responsibility as an
individual**.

If developed, the record should distinguish carefully between:

-   what the Swordmaster directly taught Vaelyn;
-   what the Swordmaster said about her people;
-   what Vaelyn personally witnessed;
-   what Vaelyn later reconstructed;
-   and what later practitioners added.

Vaelyn must never falsely present herself as having been born into the
lost culture.

A possible long-term role is that Vaelyn becomes the first custodian of
the tradition after its original culture has ended and may eventually
teach others while explicitly acknowledging where the tradition came
from.

The exact institution, terminology, ethics, succession rules, and
cultural boundaries remain **TBD**.

# 17. Continuity Guardrails

The following should be preserved in future development:

-   Vaelyn is **Valdari**, not an elf, halfling, gnome, or child-sized
    human.
-   Her individual appearance and body type do not define Valdari women
    generally.
-   Her current height is approximately **3'2" (97 cm)**.
-   She retains mature adult proportions despite her youthful Valdari
    appearance.
-   Her sword is a **literal katana**, not merely a katana-like fantasy
    sword.
-   Her commissioned katana is her fixed personal weapon design and must
    be visually reproduced from its AVR when materially visible.
-   Her Swordmaster also uses a katana, but the master's weapon is
    independent and need not resemble Vaelyn's.
-   Vaelyn's katana was commissioned specifically for her scale and
    technique.
-   The sword's speed in her hands is not explained by a special
    lightweight magical metal.
-   Vaelyn retains her druidic abilities after becoming a swordswoman.
-   A druidic staff may be appropriate to earlier-period depictions but
    should not replace the commissioned katana as her characteristic
    weapon in later-period depictions.
-   Her family alchemical background is a family influence, not a
    Valdari rule that children inherit their family's profession.
-   Sword mastery around age 50 and Valdari adulthood around age 50 are
    independent milestones.
-   The Swordmaster's lost-culture role remains **Concept** until
    deliberately adopted.
-   The family-revelation incident remains **Concept** until
    deliberately adopted.
-   Do not use Living Magic or Song-Magic as part of Vaelyn's druidry;
    those directions have been rejected from current Valdari lore.
